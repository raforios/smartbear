About me

Twenty-six years turning technical problems into business results

Systems Engineer with a Master's in Intelligent Flexible Systems and over fifteen years in the backend. I command Python, Java and JavaScript, I have built and operated infrastructure on AWS, and today I embed artificial intelligence into enterprise and government solutions. I founded BearSoft, my software and data analytics consultancy.

What sets me apart is having sat in all four seats: I build the systems, audit them under ISO 27000 and COBIT 5, train the teams that maintain them, and have run the IT departments that answer for them. That is why what I deliver survives the audit, the handover and the year after.

  • Robust, scalable APIs, with performance and security as a requirement rather than an afterthought.
  • Automated deployments (CI/CD) and infrastructure as code: less downtime and a smaller cloud bill.
  • Data turned into decisions: dashboards and reports that answer business questions rather than decorate.
  • Machine learning and generative AI models embedded in the real workflow, with their error measured and published.
  • Training for your team: university lecturer and corporate trainer since 2004.
